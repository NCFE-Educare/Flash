---
description: Standard team procedure for drafting professional emails.
---

When the user asks you to draft, write, or create an email (for any recipient or purpose), you MUST:
1. Delegate the email drafting to the `email_drafter` subagent.
2. Provide the subagent with: recipient, purpose (e.g., follow-up, introduction, request), and desired tone (formal, casual, friendly).
3. Once the subagent returns the draft, present it to the user in a clear format with Subject and Body sections.
